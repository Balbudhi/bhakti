## Key source decisions

- Kanakadhārā: the revised opening retains the bodily image and connects its delayed subject: Lakṣmī’s glance is the bee, Hari’s body the tamāla tree, and its hairs rising in delight the buds. [Chinmaya’s Sanskrit/IAST witness](https://media-cmwrc.chinmayadc.org/dev108/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/10122904/KanakadhaaraStotram.pdf) was inspected visually. Local Hālāyudha pulaka and Aṣṭādhyāyī witnesses were checked against raw sources. Thirty-three English definitions in the hymn change; iṣṭāviśiṣṭamatayaḥ remains unresolved. Source/performance-order inversions in l063–065 and l077–080 are not claimed fully resolved by synonym changes.
- Sāī ārati: [Sansthan’s printed Namaskārāṣṭaka](https://sai.org.in/sites/default/files/Namaskarashtaka_0.pdf) has गळाया, the purpose form “so that it falls away.” The inherited split गळा या invented throat-seizing. The correction follows Marathi syntax, not an English translator’s preference. लोकांपरी is “like people.” Source letters and timings are unchanged.
- Prabh Ju: [Dasam Granth Ang 710](https://sridasam.org/0710.html) and [SGGS Academy commentary](https://sikhibookclub.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/click-here-to-download-shabad-hazare-sggs-academy.pdf), printed p27, support mān as pride with mardan understood. The king Māndhātā identification was unsupported. The alternative attachment to Madhu’s pride remains explained in the note.
- Māṇḍūkya 12: the local GRETIL Upaniṣad and Śaṅkara commentary support the contextual explanation of the fourth, beyond everyday dealings, and the stilling of appearances. Śiva is “auspicious” here, not a deity-name claim. The commentary witness is unproofread, not a critical edition.
- Prajñāvivardhana: local Monier-Williams śabdabrahman (p1053 col1) and Apte support “the sacred Word,” explained as the Veda/Brahman in the gloss. The adjacent siddhasārasvata segmentation remains open.
- Tagore: two definitions in Sakatore, two in Sawan and one in E Barashe received concrete repairs; other wording was retained. Reputation or poetic density alone did not trigger rewrites.
- The channel promotion stored in Kyun Nahin Manegi is absent from SONG_SEQUENCE, so it is not a public performed occurrence. No trim was inferred from an unused definition.
