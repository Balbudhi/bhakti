## Resolved lines

- **kakad-aarti line-116.** The earlier text, “padya śloka ye do kṛṣṇa vasko haiṁ gāte”, made a person named Vasko. The Marathi original (the library’s own madhyahna-aarti line-047) reads देशिल तरि दे त्वद्यश कृष्णाने गावे, and the two 21 November hearings already heard “tvadyaśa de do”. Local MMS acoustic scoring of the native audio prefers “tvadyaśa de do” to “padya śloka ye do” in every crop tested (46.1 against 54.6 with the onset included; lower is better), and Whisper large-v3 independently heard “…शा दे दो कृष्ण…”. The short run before “haiṁ” is heard as vasko, vaspo or vasto. “usko” is the best-scoring reading that is Hindi and grammatical, and it resumes the request’s object. The note records that limit. English: “Grant Your glory; Krishna sings of it.”
- **main-kismat-ka-mara v2-2.** Two earlier hearings, Whisper and MMS all support the sounds “jo kahā vā kā kuch to sunāe”; proposed replacements scored far worse. Only the word division changes. “jo kahā … vā kā” is the Braj correlative “what was said … of that”; “kahavā” as a noun for sayings was unsupported. English: “Holding hope that someone will let me hear at least something of what was said, I sit waiting.”
