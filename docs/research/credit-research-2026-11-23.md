# Credit research — 23 November 2026

Owner request: no bare names; find and attribute missing poets and singers.

Evidence order: official release role credits (label metadata); identity of our recording established by matching its audio against the official preview, or by exact duration plus title and singer; signatures in the sung text. Voice comparison was used only inside one album or against many songs of one singer. A distributor’s artist name is not evidence of the voice: six Satpathy songs so labelled hold at least three voices. `Traditional` requires an official traditional credit or an established liturgical/traditional text. Unknown roles stay blank.
