Owner request: no bare names; find and attribute missing poets and singers.

Evidence order: official release role credits (label metadata); identity of our recording established by matching its audio against the official preview, or by exact duration plus title and singer; signatures in the sung text. Voice comparison was used only inside one album or against many songs of one singer. A distributor’s artist name is not evidence of the voice: six Satpathy songs so labelled hold at least three voices. The owner rejected a “Traditional” label on 2026-11-23: attribution follows the tradition, and an unnamed poet is left empty. Unknown roles stay blank.
