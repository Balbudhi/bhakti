# Sai Āratī source notes

The composite Madhyāhna, Dhūp, and Śej song pages use the exact Pramod Medhi
recordings published by Wings Music. Section boundaries follow the independently
audited performance order. Poet labels are component-level evidence, not an
album-wide writer claim.

Attribution policy:

- `Ghālin Loṭāṅgaṇ` remains Sant Nāmdev's work; its Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa names are not
  rewritten as Sai.
- `Āratī Jñānarājācī` and `Āratī Tukārāmācī` remain directed to Jñāneśvar and
  Tukārām.
- `Śirḍī Mājhe Paṇḍharpur` receives a component note because it recasts
  Paṇḍharpur/Viṭṭhala imagery for Sai worship.
- Exact Sai-name substitutions in Tukārām-derived Śej components receive the
  line badge; the badge does not imply that Tukārām wrote the substituted name.
- Conflicting or unsupported poet claims remain `Poet unresolved` rather than
  being inferred from commercial release metadata.
