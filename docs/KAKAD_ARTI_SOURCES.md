manual editorial metadata; they are not inferred by the transcription model.

## Adaptation markers

Only exact displayed response lines supported by comparison are marked `Sai
adaptation`:

- sequence 78 and 87: the paired `Sadguru` response within Sant Janābāī's
  `Tujhe Kyā Dū̃ Sā̃vare`;
- sequence 105, 108, 110, and 112: the explicit Sai/Bābā response lines in
  Kṛṣṇa Jogīśvara Bhīṣma's `Pañcāratī` passage.

The badge means that this displayed line is the Sai form used in the recording.
It does not attribute the substituted divine name to the original poet. When a
component author was not established confidently, the public notice omits the
poet rather than guessing.

## Vedic texts and the Sai dedication

The short sequence often called “Vedic Benedictions” is not one undifferentiated
hymn. It is the post-ārati *mantrapuṣpāñjali* sequence, here cited on the page
as its distinct source passages:

- sequence 185–186: the closing verse of the *Puruṣa Sūkta*, Ṛgveda 10.90.16;
  `Hariḥ Oṃ` is a recitational opening, not part of the Ṛgvedic verse. Text
  cross-check: https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV10
- sequence 187–189: the Vaiśravaṇa/Kubera mantra, Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 1.31.6;
  the opening `Oṃ` is liturgical. Text cross-check:
  https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_veda/taittirIyaAraNyaka.html
- sequence 190–193: the royal benediction and the Marutta verse, Aitareya
  Brāhmaṇa 8.15. The page’s note distinguishes this Vedic quotation from its
  following Sai lines.
- sequence 194–195: a Sai dedication and acclamation added by this liturgical
  sequence. These two displayed lines are marked `Sai adaptation`; they are
  not attributed to the preceding Vedic text.

## Aisā Yeī Bā Sāī Digambarā

The refrain says `Sāī digambarā`: Sai is directly addressed as *Digambara*
(sky-clad ascetic). `Anasūyā-Atri-kumārā` in the next verse is the traditional
Dattātreya identification, not a correction of the refrain into “Datta
Digambara.” The Shree Saibaba Sansthan Trust lists *Aisa Yei Ba* as a distinct
ārati component: https://sai.org.in/en/aarties . Its explanatory account
explicitly connects the invocation’s Datta imagery with Sai while preserving
that direct wording: https://sai.org.in/sites/default/files/2006a-page-1-10.pdf
